## Live acceptance still required

- Solo normal fight: verify both glyph elements, one/two waves, fire-lane movement, flare soak/no-heal, meteor movement lock/burn, and all three Shadow Crash orientations.
- Duo normal fight: verify one glyph activation protects only the two participants, hazards never affect spectators/other instances, and each Shadow Crash follows its own player.
- Representative contract: verify increased lane/wave density, transition cleanup, and no stale helpers after death/logout/re-entry.

Automated and cache validation can prove state ownership, geometry, timing, and mapped definitions, but it cannot substitute for those client-visible live checks.

## Final implementation amendment (2026-09-04)

This amendment supersedes older status text above where the implementation has since changed.

- Judge NPC `14180` is size 3 in the revision-240 cache. West and east southwest anchors now mirror the complete occupied footprint, placing the east Judge at local `x=50` instead of the former incorrect `x=52`.
- Accepted Judge hits keep the forced final damage on the visible hitsplat and synchronize the Judge NPC, shared 400 HP, and every participant HUD from the final processed impact. Blocked styles remain true zero-damage hits. Judge-only melee AP range is now bound into the normal collision and line-of-sight router after the stepping-stone crossing.
- The hit framework has no impact-cycle key available while target-side hit modifiers run. Same-tick multi-hit limiting therefore remains submission-tick based and is not claimed as exact for differently delayed hits that converge on one impact tick.
- The records option, account-persistent kill count/personal best/recent-four average, cache-backed imp `14181`, presentation dialogue, and destruction cleanup are implemented. Exact native scoreboard presentation audio and unobserved imp timing remain live-validation gaps.
- Contract selection is leader-owned and snapshotted for the eligible party. Explicit rules now cover solo/duo eligibility, one-item contract entry, the 100-noted-pineapple-pizza custom entry, supplier-only return-on-victory, acquisition profiles, restoration reduction, Familiar consumable restrictions and doubled special regeneration, Sensory attack-range reduction, Glyphic prayer-bonus inversion, and Bloodied minimum hits.
- Familiar's 1/100 pet benefit is rolled once through the independent tertiary path; it is no longer also dispatched as a guaranteed contract reward.
- Persistent Oathplate/Harmony shadow pools remain unimplemented because the supplied capture establishes the `3252` Shadow Crash family but not a distinct persistent-pool asset, radius, damage cadence, or expiry. Death Charge credit for transition-cleared flares remains blocked by the absence of a server-side Death Charge effect/cooldown callback.
- Native Death's Coffer, gravestone UI, and timed-transfer services are absent from the current tree. Voice-side persistent storage and atomic inventory-then-bank coin reclaim are implemented without inventing those missing systems.
- Fixed Judge camera coordinates and complete Yama sound IDs are not established by the supplied decoded evidence and were not guessed.
